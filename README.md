Stitchify ✂️
Stitchify is an on-demand, doorstep tailoring platform that connects customers with skilled local tailors. By combining traditional craftsmanship with modern convenience, Stitchify removes the hassle of visiting a physical market by bringing verified tailors straight to the customer's doorstep.

🚀 Features
Bespoke Doorstep Service: Tailors come directly to you for measurements and consultations.

Verified Craftspeople: Access to a network of expert tailors with years of traditional and modern stitching experience.

Easy 2-Minute Booking: Quick and seamless online scheduling for tailoring slots.

Transparent Ratings: Customer reviews and ratings for every tailor to ensure confidence and quality.

Dual-Faceted Platform: Built for both Customers looking for perfect custom fits and Tailors/Partners looking to scale their business and manage bookings.

🧵 Services Offered
Stitchify provides a wide variety of custom tailoring and alteration services:

Traditional & Festive Wear: Kurtas, Saree & Blouse, Bridal Lehenga

Western & Formals: Shirts, Pants, Suits & Trousers

Alterations: Custom adjustments for existing wardrobe items

📦 Getting Started
For Customers
Navigate to the landing page.

Click on Book a Tailor Free or Join Free.

Select your desired service and schedule an available slot.

For Tailor Partners
Click on Join as Partner in the tailors section or footer.

Register your shop and list your specialization services to start accepting local bookings.
